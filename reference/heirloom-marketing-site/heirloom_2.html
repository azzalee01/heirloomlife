The platform

Your life's work deserves a living plan.

Most people treat estate planning as a one-time task — a document filed and forgotten. Heirloom is different. It's a living platform that stays current as your life evolves, so your intentions are always reflected accurately.

Your wealth picture
A complete, organised view of everything you've built — property, superannuation, investments, business interests, digital assets. The clarity most people never have about their own financial life.
Your circle of trust
The people who matter, their roles, and their relationship to what you've built. Add, update, and manage your people as your life changes — always current, always intentional.
Your instructions
A legally binding expression of your intentions, generated from your actual life picture and reviewed by a lawyer. Updated as you are — not a static document you'll never look at again.
Quarterly design review
Every quarter, Heirloom checks in. Does your plan still reflect your life? Bought a property, had a child, started a business — your platform prompts you before life moves ahead of your intentions.
Executor continuity
When something happens, the people you trust have exactly what they need — instantly accessible, clearly organised, no searching, no delays. Your intentions carried out precisely as designed.
Digital asset vault
Account credentials, subscriptions, crypto, online accounts — the part of your life most people completely overlook. Documented, stored securely, accessible to the right people at the right time.
