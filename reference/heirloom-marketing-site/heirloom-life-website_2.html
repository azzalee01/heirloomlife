The Folder

Something to hold,
not just a login to remember.

Every Heirloom member receives a cloth-bound folder — a permanent shell that houses a replaceable Will insert, updated by mail every time your Vault changes. Inside, a wax-sealed Legacy Key holds the code your executor will need, the day it's needed.

The folder is permanent — only the insert is replaced as your Will is amended.

No QR codes, no links to expire — the Legacy Key is a physical, alphanumeric record.
