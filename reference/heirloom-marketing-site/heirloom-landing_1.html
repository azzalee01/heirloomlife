Questions

What people
ask first.

Is a will made through Heirloom legally valid?+
Yes. Every Heirloom will is reviewed by a qualified Australian solicitor and prepared to meet the Wills Act requirements in your state or territory. You sign it in front of two witnesses — which is all that's required to make it legally binding.
How is this different from a DIY will kit?+
A DIY kit gives you a blank form. Heirloom gives you a guided experience, a lawyer's eyes on your document, and ongoing storage and updates. The difference between a form and a finished will that actually reflects your life.
What if my situation is complicated?+
Heirloom is designed for modern Australian lives — blended families, superannuation nominations, digital assets, interstate property, business interests. If your situation is genuinely complex, we'll connect you with a specialist solicitor at no extra cost.
Can I update my will later?+
Yes — and you should. Life changes. Your Individual plan includes one free update within 12 months; the Couple's plan includes unlimited updates for 24 months. After that, updates are $49 per change.
How long does it take?+
Most people complete their guided questionnaire in 20–30 minutes. Solicitor review is typically returned within one business day. Then you sign, witness, and you're done.
