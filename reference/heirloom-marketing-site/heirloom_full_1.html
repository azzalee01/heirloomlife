The platform

Your life's work deserves a living plan.

Most people treat estate planning as a one-time task — a document filed and forgotten. Heirloom Life is different. A living platform that stays current as your life evolves.

Your wealth picture
Property, superannuation, investments, business interests, digital assets — organised in one living picture. The clarity most people never have about their own financial life.
Your circle of trust
The people who matter, their roles, their relationship to what you've built. Add, update, and manage as your life changes — always current, always intentional.
Your instructions
A legally binding expression of your intentions, reviewed by a lawyer, generated from your actual life picture. Updated as you are.
Quarterly design review
Every quarter, Heirloom Life checks in. Does your plan still reflect your life? Life event prompts before life moves ahead of your intentions.
Executor continuity
When something happens, the people you trust have exactly what they need — instantly accessible, clearly organised, no searching, no delays.
Digital asset vault
Accounts, crypto, subscriptions — documented, stored securely, accessible to the right people at the right time.
